    Quick Safety Tips

    Earthquake
    Drop, Cover, Hold On. Stay away from windows. Go outside only when shaking stops.
    Tsunami
    If near the coast and you feel a strong quake, move to high ground immediately.
    Flood
    Move to higher floors. Never walk or drive through floodwater.
    Cyclone
    Shelter indoors away from windows. Keep a battery radio and emergency kit.
